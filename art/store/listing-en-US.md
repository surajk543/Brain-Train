## Full description (max 4000)
Train your brain with a calm, beautiful twist on Mahjong solitaire.

Tap a free tile and it drops into your tray. Find its twin and the pair strikes and clears. Fill the tray without a match and the round is over — so plan a few moves ahead, and keep your tray clear.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap any free tile: one that is not covered and has an open left or right side.
• Each tapped tile goes into the tray at the top. Two matching tiles in the tray clear together.
• Clear every pair on the board to win the level.
• Tray full? Revive to put the tiles back on the board, or start the level again.

FEATURES
• 100 levels, from relaxed (1/10) to fiendish (10/10).
• Trays of 2 to 5 slots: the harder the level, the less room for mistakes.
• More than 300 hand-picked tile pictures, many of them animated.
• Combos and speed bonuses: match quickly to multiply your score.
• Every level is proven solvable.
• A peaceful night garden with gentle music and sound.
• Levels download in the background, and the ones on your phone play offline.
• Completed Levels: replay any level to beat your best time and score.

Brain Train is free to play. It shows a short video ad only when you choose to revive a game.
