Privacy Policy

Brain Train · Sun Game Studio
Effective 16 October 2026 · Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what Brain Train collects, why, and what it does not collect. It describes the app as it actually behaves. If we change what the app collects, we change this page and the date above with it.

In short: Brain Train has no account and no sign-up. Your progress stays on your device. The app downloads levels and pictures from our content server. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one to revive a game, and they are served by Google AdMob. If you say you are under 13, ads are child-directed and not personalized. Google Play Games sign-in is optional.

1. Who we are

Brain Train is a Mahjong tile-matching puzzle game published by Sun Game Studio, Bengaluru, India. For anything in this policy, write to contact@sungamestudio.com.

2. What stays on your device

WhatWhy
Your game progress: levels won, best times, scores and combos, and the game you left unfinished So you can continue where you stopped and replay levels
Your settings (sound, vibration)To remember your choices
Your answer to the age question, kept only as "under 13" or "13 and over" (not your birth year) To choose which kind of ads may be shown (section 4)
Downloaded levels and picturesSo levels you already have play without internet

None of this is sent to us. We have no server that stores players' data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

3. Level downloads

The app downloads level files and their pictures from our content server, which runs on Cloudflare. Like any website, the server receives your IP address and basic request details (for example the file asked for and the app's network library). We use these only to deliver the files; we do not combine them with anything else or use them to identify you. Cloudflare processes them under its privacy policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

When a game ends with a full tray, you can tap Revive to watch a rewarded video ad and get your tiles back. Ads are shown only then, and only when you choose to watch. They are served by Google AdMob.

To show and measure an ad and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK collects information from your device, such as the device model and operating system, your IP address (and from it an approximate location), ad interactions and, for players aged 13 and over, the device's advertising ID. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Under 13 (from the age question): every ad request is marked as child-directed and as for a user under the age of consent, ads are limited to content rated G, and ads are not personalized. This follows Google Play's Families policy.
  • 13 and over: ads are rated up to PG. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Google settings.

5. Google Play Games (optional, Android)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, Brain Train can show your achievements and leaderboards. Your Play Games profile and the scores and achievements the game reports are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address. You can play without signing in.

6. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address or phone number.
  • No location permission, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, and no crash-reporting service.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment details.
  • No chat or other content shared with other players.

7. Children

Brain Train is suitable for all ages, including children. At first launch it asks for the player's birth year with a neutral question. If the answer is under 13, the app treats the player as a child: ads are child-directed, non-personalized and rated G (section 4). The app collects no personal information from children for itself. Parents or guardians who have a question can write to contact@sungamestudio.com.

8. Your rights and choices

Because your progress stays on your device, you control it: clear the app's data or uninstall the app to delete it. You can change your ad consent in Settings → Privacy choices (where it applies) and your advertising ID in your phone's settings. For data that Google processes for ads or Play Games, see Google's privacy policy. You can also write to us with any request; we answer within 30 days. These rights apply wherever you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU and UK GDPR.

9. Changes

When the app starts collecting something new, or stops, we update this page and the dates at the top.
